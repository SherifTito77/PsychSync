## Detailed Sales Playbook

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**Company Characteristics:**
- Size: 500-5000 employees (Phase 1-2), 5000+ (Phase 3)
- Revenue: $50M-1B annually
- Industries: Technology, Healthcare, Professional Services, Financial Services
- HR Team: 5-50 people with dedicated tech budget
- Digital transformation initiatives underway

**Pain Points:**
- Team performance and productivity challenges
- Remote/hybrid work team management
- Employee engagement and retention
- Leadership development and succession planning
- Data-driven decision making needs

**Decision Makers:**
- VP of HR / CHRO (Primary)
- Head of Talent Development
- COO / Head of Operations
- Department Leaders (Technology, Customer Success)
- CEO (for strategic implementations)

### Sales Process Methodology

#### Stage 1: Discovery & Qualification (Days 1-15)
**Key Activities:**
- Initial qualification call (30 minutes)
- Pain point identification
- Budget and timeline confirmation
- Stakeholder mapping
- Technical requirements assessment

**Qualification Criteria:**
- Annual HR tech budget >$50K
- Clear pain points in team management
- Timeline <6 months for implementation
- Decision-maker involvement confirmed

**Tools & Resources:**
- Discovery call script
- Qualification scoring rubric
- Stakeholder mapping template
- Budget confirmation framework

#### Stage 2: Solution Design (Days 16-45)
**Key Activities:**
- Deep dive assessment needs analysis
- Solution design and customization
- ROI analysis and business case
- Technical feasibility assessment
- Proof of concept planning

**Deliverables:**
- Custom solution proposal
- ROI analysis and business case
- Implementation timeline
- Proof of concept plan
- Technical integration requirements

**Success Metrics:**
- Stakeholder alignment confirmed
- Business case approved
- Technical feasibility validated
- Pilot program approved

#### Stage 3: Pilot Implementation (Days 46-90)
**Key Activities:**
- Pilot team setup and configuration
- User training and onboarding
- Assessment completion and data collection
- Results analysis and review
- Executive presentation and Q&A

**Pilot Success Criteria:**
- 80%+ team participation rate
- Measurable improvements in team metrics
- Positive user feedback (NPS >40)
- Clear ROI demonstrated

#### Stage 4: Full Implementation (Days 91-180)
**Key Activities:**
- Enterprise-wide rollout
- Advanced features implementation
- Integration with existing systems
- Custom reporting and analytics
- Change management and training

**Implementation Timeline:**
- Phase 1: Core functionality (30 days)
- Phase 2: Advanced features (45 days)
- Phase 3: Integrations and customizations (60 days)
- Phase 4: Optimization and expansion (45 days)

#### Stage 5: Value Realization & Expansion (Days 181+)
**Key Activities:**
- Business impact measurement
- Expansion opportunities identification
- Advanced features upsell
- Case study development
- Referral generation

**Expansion Opportunities:**
- Additional departments or business units

## Risk Mitigation & Contingency Plans

### Market Risks
**Risk:** Established competitors (SHL, Hogan) defend market share
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on AI-powered differentiation
- Target underserved mid-market segment
- Emphasize team optimization vs. individual assessment
- Build strong partnerships for channel advantage

**Risk:** Economic downturn reduces HR tech budgets
**Mitigation:**
- Emphasize ROI and cost savings
- Offer flexible pricing and payment terms
- Focus on essential compliance and risk management needs
- Provide implementation services to offset budget constraints

### Execution Risks
**Risk:** Slow customer acquisition
**Mitigation:**
- Diversify lead generation channels
- Increase marketing spend and optimization
- Leverage partnerships for channel sales
- Expand sales team more aggressively

**Risk:** High customer churn
**Mitigation:**
- Invest heavily in customer success
- Implement comprehensive onboarding
- Develop proactive retention programs
- Build strong customer community

### Product Risks
**Risk:** Technical issues or bugs affect adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Comprehensive testing and QA processes
- Staged rollout and pilot programs
- 24/7 monitoring and support
- Rapid bug fixing and hotfix capabilities
