# PsychSync Pricing Strategy

**Good/Better/Best tier framework and monetization approach**

---

## 📋 Executive Summary

PsychSync uses a tiered SaaS pricing model aligned with user personas and organizational maturity. This document defines our pricing philosophy, tier structures, packaging strategy, and monetization tactics.

**Pricing Philosophy**: Value-based, persona-aligned, expansion revenue focused
**Current Model**: Free / Professional / Enterprise
**Target LTV:CAC Ratio**: 3:1
**Target Net Revenue Retention**: > 120%

---

## 🎯 Pricing Principles

### Core Principles
1. **Value-Based Alignment**: Price tiers map to clear value delivery
2. **Persona-Driven Packaging**: Features match user needs (Individual → Enterprise)
3. **Freemium as Growth Engine**: Free tier drives acquisition and word-of-mouth
4. **Expansion Revenue**: Upsell and cross-sell prioritized over new logo acquisition
5. **Annual Commitment Incentives**: Discount for annual prepay (10-20%)
6. **Transparent Pricing**: Public pricing for Free and Professional, custom for Enterprise

### Anti-Patterns We Avoid
- ❌ Feature gating that creates "crippled" free tier
- ❌ Complex, confusing pricing structures
- ❌ Frequent price changes that erode trust
- ❌ Hidden fees or surprise charges
- ❌ Pricing misalignment with customer willingness-to-pay

## 💰 Current Pricing Tiers

### 🆓 **Free Tier** (Individual)

**Target**: Individual professionals, students, solo users
**Pricing**: $0/month
**Goal**: Product-market fit discovery, user growth, brand awareness

| Feature | Included |
|---------|----------|
| **Assessments** | All assessment types (Big Five, MBTI, Enneagram, etc.) |
| **Team Size** | 1 user only |
| **Assessment Limit** | 3 assessments total |
| **Analytics** | Personal results only |
| **AI Insights** | Basic personal insights |
| **Reports** | Personal PDF only |
| **History Retention** | 90 days |
| **Support** | Community only |
| **Integrations** | None |

**Limitations (Create Upgrade Urgency)**:
- Cannot create teams or invite others
- No team analytics or comparison
- No data export
- 90-day data retention (lose data after)
- No API access

**Monetization Strategy**:
- **Conversion Triggers**:
  - "Want to compare with your team?" → Upgrade to Professional
  - "Your data will be deleted in X days" → Upgrade to keep data
  - "Unlock advanced AI insights" → Upgrade to Professional
- **Time-Limit Trial**: Consider 14-day "Pro trial" with full features

**Success Metrics**:
- Free → Paid conversion: 5-8%
- Word-of-mouth rate: 30% of new sign-ups from referrals
- Free user DAU/MAU: > 20%

**Pricing**: $99/month or $990/year (save 17%)
**Goal**: Primary revenue driver, growth engine

|---------|----------|

| **Team Size** | Up to 50 users |
| **Analytics** | Team analytics, comparisons, trends |
| **AI Insights** | Advanced team insights, recommendations |
| **Reports** | Team reports, custom reports, scheduled reports |
| **History Retention** | 1 year |

| **Integrations** | Slack, Microsoft Teams |
| **Assessment Reminders** | Automated team reminders |
| **Custom Branding** | Basic (logo on reports) |

**Additional Pricing**:
- **Overage**: $3/user/month for teams > 50 users
- **Add-on**: +$49/month for priority support (4-hour response)

**Monetization Strategy**:
- **Expansion Paths**:
  - Team grows beyond 50 users → Surcharges or upgrade to Enterprise
  - Need SSO/security → Upgrade to Enterprise

  - Need API access → Upgrade to Enterprise

**Success Metrics**:
- Professional tier revenue: 60% of total ARR
- Average team size: 25 users
- Expansion revenue: 20% of Professional ARR from upsells
- Renewal rate: > 80%

### 🏢 **Enterprise Tier** (Organization)

**Target**: Large organizations (100+ employees), enterprises, consultants
**Pricing**: Custom quote (typically $10K-$100K+ annually)
**Goal**: High-value contracts, strategic accounts, reference customers

| Feature | Included |
|---------|----------|
| **Assessments** | All assessment types, unlimited custom assessments |
| **Team Size** | Unlimited users |
| **Analytics** | Organization-wide, advanced analytics, custom dashboards |
| **AI Insights** | All AI features, succession planning, predictive analytics |
| **Reports** | White-label, executive reports, API access |
| **History Retention** | Unlimited |
| **Support** | Dedicated success manager, 4-hour response |
| **Integrations** | Full integration hub, HRIS, BI tools, API access |
| **Security** | SSO/SAML, advanced security, SOC2 compliance |
| **Custom Contracts** | Flexible terms, SLAs, legal review |
| **Implementation** | White-glove onboarding, training, change management |

**Pricing Factors**:
- **Per-Seat Pricing**: $8-15/user/month (volume discounts)
- **Platform Fee**: $2K-5K/month minimum
- **Implementation Fee**: $5K-25K one-time (depends on scope)
- **Premium Support**: +20% for 24/7 support, dedicated engineer

**Monetization Strategy**:
- **Multi-Year Contracts**: 2-3 year commitments with 10-15% discount
- **Expansion Revenue**:
  - Add departments/business units
  - Expand usage (more assessments, more features)
  - Professional services (training, consulting)
- **Renewal Strategy**: 90%+ renewal target, price increases capped at 5-10%

**Success Metrics**:
- Enterprise tier revenue: 30% of total ARR
- Average contract value: $50K+ annually
- Expansion revenue: 25% of Enterprise ARR from expansions
- Renewal rate: > 90%
- Logo value: Use as case studies and references

## 🚀 Go-to-Market Pricing Tactics

### Acquisition Pricing

#### **Self-Serve (Free → Professional)**
- **Landing Page**: Clear pricing page with feature comparison
- **Trial Offers**: 14-day free trial of Professional features
- **First-Time Discounts**: 20% off first year (common for SaaS)
- **Student/Education Discounts**: 50% off for verified students/educators
- **Non-Profit Discounts**: 30% off for registered 501(c)(3)

#### **Sales-Assisted (Enterprise)**
- **Custom Quotes**: Tailored pricing based on organization size and needs
- **Volume Discounts**: Tiered pricing breaks at 100, 500, 1000 users
- **Multi-Year Discounts**: 10% off for 2-year, 15% for 3-year commitments
- **Payment Terms**: Net 30 for established companies, prepaid for startups
- **Proof of Concept**: 30-day pilot with reduced implementation fee

### Expansion Pricing

#### **Upsell Triggers**
| Trigger | Current Tier | Upgrade To | Incentive |
|---------|--------------|------------|-----------|
| Team > 50 users | Professional | Enterprise | Waive implementation fee |
| Need SSO | Professional | Enterprise | 3 months free |
| Want API | Professional | Enterprise | API credit |
| Consultant use | Professional | Consultant | 2 months free |
| Data export needed | Free | Professional | 50% off first month |

#### **Cross-Sell Opportunities**
- **Professional Services**: Training ($5K), Change Management ($10K), Custom Assessment Development ($15K)
- **Premium Support**: 24/7 support for +20% of contract value
- **Advisory Services**: Quarterly business review with product team for $5K/quarter

### Retention Pricing

#### **Renewal Incentives**
- **Early Renewal Discount**: 15% off for renewing 30 days before expiration
- **Multi-Year Renewal**: 20% off for 2-year renewal
- **Expansion Bundles**: Discount for adding features at renewal

#### **Churn Recovery**
- **Downgrade Offers**: Can't afford Enterprise? Downgrade to Professional at 10% discount
- **Pause Option**: For temporary hardship (max 3 months, available for Professional+)
- **Exit Interviews**: Offer 50% discount if willing to provide detailed feedback

## 🎯 Pricing Experiment Framework

### A/B Test Ideas

#### **Test 1: Free Trial Length**
- **Control**: 14-day Professional trial
- **Variant A**: 30-day trial
- **Variant B**: 7-day trial with email course
- **Metric**: Trial → Paid conversion rate

#### **Test 2: Annual Discount**
- **Control**: 17% discount (current pricing: $990 vs $1188)
- **Variant A**: 20% discount ($950 vs $1188)
- **Variant B**: 25% discount ($890 vs $1188)
- **Metric**: Annual plan adoption rate

#### **Test 3: Tier Name Impact**
- **Control**: Free / Professional / Enterprise
- **Variant A**: Starter / Growth / Scale
- **Variant B**: Individual / Team / Organization
- **Metric**: Click-through to pricing page, conversion

#### **Test 4: Anchoring Effect**
- **Control**: Show Professional ($99) with Enterprise "Contact Us"
- **Variant A**: Show all three prices with Enterprise at $499/mo starting point
- **Variant B**: Show Professional as "Most Popular" with badge
- **Metric**: Professional vs. Enterprise selection

### Pricing Health Indicators

**Green Flags** 🟢:
- Net revenue retention > 110%
- Average contract value increasing
- Expansion revenue > new logo revenue
- Low price resistance in sales calls

**Yellow Flags** 🟡:
- High discounting (>20% off list price)

**Red Flags** 🔴:
- Declining average contract value

- Competitor underbidding consistently
- Negative ROI perception in win/loss analysis

## 🔄 Pricing Maintenance & Evolution

### Quarterly Pricing Review
1. **Analyze Metrics**: Review conversion, ASP, churn, expansion
2. **Competitive Analysis**: Check competitor pricing changes
3. **Customer Feedback**: Review pricing feedback from sales/CS
4. **Market Positioning**: Ensure we're positioned correctly
5. **Adjust if Needed**: Small adjustments (<10%) to optimize

### Annual Pricing Strategy Review
1. **Value Delivered**: Are we delivering more value than 12 months ago?
2. **Willingness-to-Pay**: Research and customer interviews
3. **Price Increase Consideration**: 5-10% increase for new customers
4. **Grandfathering**: Decide whether to increase existing customers
5. **Communicate Changes**: 60-day notice for any pricing changes

### When to Raise Prices
- ✅ Major feature release (AI capabilities, new modules)
- ✅ Proven ROI with case studies and metrics
- ✅ Product maturity and established value
- ✅ Competitive differentiation validated
- ✅ Low price sensitivity (<10% win/loss due to price)

### When to Lower Prices
- ❌ **Almost never** - signals desperation
- Exception: Competitive threat from superior alternative at lower price
- Exception: Pricing error (too high for market)
- Alternative: Keep price, add more value instead

## 🎯 Pricing Communications

### Sales Enablement Talking Points

**Value-Based Selling Scripts**:
- "For $99/month, you're getting a full HR analytics platform that would cost $50K+ to build internally"
- "Our customers typically see 10x ROI within 6 months through improved team performance and retention"
- "The average team lead saves 5 hours per week on team analytics and reporting"

**Objection Handling**:
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "Let's calculate ROI together. What's the cost of a bad hire? Team conflict? Turnover?" |
| "Can you discount?" | "We offer 10% discount for annual prepay. Beyond that, let's discuss removing features to lower cost" |
| "Why not free?" | "Our free tier is perfect for individuals. For teams, the value is in shared insights and analytics" |
| "Competitor is cheaper" | "True, but we offer X, Y, Z features they don't. Which matters most for your use case?" |
