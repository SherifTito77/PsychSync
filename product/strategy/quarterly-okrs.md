## 📋 Executive Summary

This document contains quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for the PsychSync product team. OKRs align the team on high-impact outcomes, provide focus, and enable progress tracking.

**OKR Philosophy**: Ambitious goals (stretch targets) with measurable results
**Cadence**: Quarterly (Q1-Q4) with monthly check-ins
**Format**: 3-5 Objectives per quarter, 3-4 Key Results per Objective
**Success**: 60-70% achievement of stretch goals indicates healthy ambition

## 🔄 OKR Process

### Quarterly Planning (First Monday of Quarter)
1. **Review Previous Quarter**: What worked? What didn't?
2. **Set Company Objectives**: Leadership team defines 2-3 company OKRs
3. **Team OKRs**: Each team sets 3-5 OKRs aligned with company objectives
4. **Dependencies**: Identify cross-team dependencies
5. **Resource Allocation**: Confirm capacity for initiatives
6. **OKR Approval**: Leadership review and sign-off

### Monthly Check-ins (First Monday of Month)
1. **Progress Update**: Green/Yellow/Red status for each KR
2. **Adjustments**: Add/remove/edit KRs based on new information
3. **Blockers**: Identify and resolve impediments
4. **Celebrate Wins**: Recognize achievements

### Weekly Syncs (Product Team)
1. **Initiative Updates**: Status of key initiatives
2. **Metric Review**: Track leading indicators
3. **Roadmap Adjustments**: Reprioritize based on progress

### End-of-Quarter Review
1. **Score OKRs**: Final achievement percentage (0-100%)
2. **Retrospective**: What did we learn? What would we do differently?
3. **Cascade to Next Quarter**: Carry over incomplete items
4. **Document Learnings**: Update OKR playbooks

## 📏 OKR Scoring Guidelines

### Achievement Levels

| Score | Definition | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| **1.0 (100%)** | Achieved ambitious target | Increased activation from 45% to 65% (target: 65%) |
| **0.7 (70%)** | Made solid progress, fell short | Increased activation from 45% to 60% (target: 65%) |
| **0.4 (40%)** | Some progress, but far from target | Increased activation from 45% to 50% (target: 65%) |
| **0.0 (0%)** | No progress or moved backwards | Activation stayed at 45% or dropped |

### Healthy OKR Achievement

**Target Range**: 0.6 - 0.7 (60-70%)
- **Below 0.6**: OKRs may not be ambitious enough
- **Above 0.7**: Excellent execution, consider stretching further next quarter
- **Consistently 1.0**: OKRs are too easy—increase ambition

## 🎯 OKR Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- **Set ambitious targets**: 50% chance of achievement = good stretch goal
- **Make outcomes measurable**: Quantifiable KRs with clear targets
- **Limit to 3-5 objectives per quarter**: Focus over breadth
- **Align to strategy**: Every OKR should tie to company mission
- **Review regularly**: Weekly updates, monthly deep-dives
- **Learn from failure**: Missed OKRs are learning opportunities

### Don'ts ❌
- **Don't set everything as Priority 1**: If everything is priority, nothing is
- **Don't create laundry lists**: OKRs aren't task lists
- **Don't forget to measure**: KRs must be quantifiable
- **Don't set and forget**: OKRs require active management
- **Don't punish misses**: Learning is more important than hitting every target
