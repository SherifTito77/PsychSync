PsychSync is positioned to capture a significant share of the $25+ billion psychometric assessment and team management software market. With our advanced AI engine, multi-framework support, and enterprise-ready architecture, we can address critical gaps in the current market.

- **Advanced AI Engine**: Unique selling proposition vs traditional assessment tools

- Business Intelligence Tools

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Tiers

**Starter Tier ($12/user/month, minimum 25 users)**
- Basic assessments (MBTI, Big Five)
- Team dashboard and basic reporting
- Email support
- Perfect for teams getting started with psychometric assessments

**Professional Tier ($25/user/month, minimum 50 users)**
- All assessment frameworks
- Advanced team analytics and insights
- API access and integrations
- Priority support
- Ideal for growing organizations needing comprehensive insights

**Enterprise Tier ($45+/user/month, custom pricing)**
- Unlimited assessments and users
- AI-powered predictive analytics
- Custom integrations and white-labeling
- Dedicated success manager and 24/7 support
- Advanced security and compliance features

### Pricing Psychology
- Position in middle of market range ($25-30 vs. $15-50 industry range)
- Volume discounts for larger deployments
- Annual contracts with 10% discount
- Implementation fees for enterprise ($5K-15K one-time)

## 9. Success Metrics & KPIs

### Financial Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Month-over-month growth
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: Year-over-year growth
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Cost to acquire new customer
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Total revenue per customer
- **Churn Rate**: Monthly and annual customer retention
- **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)**: Revenue retention from existing customers

### Operational Metrics
- **Lead Generation**: Monthly inbound and outbound leads
- **Conversion Rate**: Lead to customer conversion percentage
- **Sales Cycle**: Average time from lead to close
- **Deal Size**: Average annual contract value
- **Expansion Revenue**: Upsell and cross-sell revenue
- **Customer Health**: Product engagement and satisfaction scores

### Product Metrics
- **User Adoption**: Active users vs. total users
- **Feature Usage**: Utilization of key features and assessments
- **Customer Satisfaction**: NPS scores and feedback ratings
- **Support Metrics**: Response time, resolution rate, customer satisfaction
