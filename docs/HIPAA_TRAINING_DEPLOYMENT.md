# 🎓 HIPAA Training Deployment Guide

**Get all staff trained and compliant in 1 week**

---

## 📋 Training Overview

**Who Must Train:** ALL staff (employees, contractors, clinicians, IT, executives)
**Duration:** 2 hours (initial), 1 hour (annual refresher)
**Passing Score:** 80% or higher
**Format:** Self-guided reading + quiz

**Training Manual:** `HIPAA_TRAINING_MANUAL.md`

---

## 🚀 7-Day Training Rollout Plan

### **Day 1: Preparation**

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Review `HIPAA_TRAINING_MANUAL.md`
- [ ] Set up tracking spreadsheet (who trained, when, score)
- [ ] Prepare acknowledgment forms
- [ ] Schedule training sessions (if doing group training)
- [ ] Send announcement email

**Email Announcement Template:**

```
Subject: MANDATORY HIPAA Training - Due by [Date]

Team,

All PsychSync staff must complete HIPAA training by [Date, 7 days from now].

This is REQUIRED for anyone who may access user health information.

**What You Need to Do:**
1. Read the HIPAA Training Manual (attached or in shared drive)
2. Complete the quiz at the end
3. Score 80% or higher
4. Sign the HIPAA Acknowledgment Form

**Time Commitment:** 2 hours

**Why This Matters:**
- We handle sensitive health information
- Legal requirement (fines up to $1.5M if we violate HIPAA)
- Protects our users and our organization

**Deadline:** Complete by [Date]

**Training Manual:** [Link to HIPAA_TRAINING_MANUAL.md]
**Quiz:** [Link to quiz form or attached document]
**Acknowledgment Form:** [Link to form or attached document]

If you have questions, contact [compliance@psychsync.ai]

Thank you for taking privacy and security seriously.

[Your Name]
```

---

### **Day 2-3: Training Period**

**What Staff Do:**
- Read `HIPAA_TRAINING_MANUAL.md` (7 modules)
- Complete final quiz (10 questions)
- Score 80% or higher to pass
- Retake if needed (unlimited attempts)

**Quiz Options:**

**Option A: Google Form**
- Create quiz in Google Forms
- Automatically grade
- Export results to spreadsheet
- Template: See below

**Option B: Paper Quiz**
- Print quiz from training manual
- Staff complete on paper
- Score manually
- File in personnel folder

**Option C: LMS (Learning Management System)**
- Upload to your HR platform
- Automatic tracking
- Issue certificates

---

### **Day 4-5: Follow-Up & Support**

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Send reminder to those who haven't completed
- [ ] Offer help sessions for questions
- [ ] Review quiz results
- [ ] Identify anyone who needs to retake

**Reminder Email Template:**

```
Subject: Reminder: HIPAA Training Due in 2 Days

Hi [Name],

Just a reminder that HIPAA training is due by [Date].

**Your Status:** [Not Started / In Progress / Quiz Completed]

**If You Haven't Started:**
- Set aside 2 hours today or tomorrow
- Read the training manual
- Take the quiz

**If You're Stuck:**
- Reply to this email with questions
- I can schedule a 15-minute call to help

**If You Completed Training:**
- Thank you! Make sure you've signed the acknowledgment form

Deadline: [Date]

[Your Name]
```

---

### **Day 6: Final Push**

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Email those who still haven't completed
- [ ] Offer one-on-one help if needed
- [ ] Extend deadline by 1-2 days if necessary (but not recommended)
- [ ] Plan for non-compliant staff (can't access PHI)

**Urgent Reminder Template:**

```
Subject: URGENT: HIPAA Training Due Tomorrow

Hi [Name],

This is your final reminder to complete HIPAA training.

**Deadline:** Tomorrow, [Date] at [time]

**Why This Matters:**
- You cannot access user data without completing training
- This is a legal requirement
- Non-compliance puts the organization at risk

**If You Need Help:**
I'm available until [time] today to answer questions or walk through the material.

[Your Name]
[Phone number]
```

---

### **Day 7: Review & Document**

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Verify all staff completed training
- [ ] Collect all signed acknowledgment forms
- [ ] Record all scores in tracking spreadsheet
- [ ] Issue completion certificates
- [ ] File acknowledgment forms in personnel folders
- [ ] Report completion status to leadership

## 🎓 Quiz Template (Google Form)

**Create a Google Form with these questions:**

**1. What is the minimum necessary standard?**
- a) Access all records to be thorough
- b) Access only the PHI needed to do your job
- c) Access any records you want as long as you're careful
- d) Only doctors need to follow this

**Answer:** b

---

**2. A user screens positive for suicide risk. Can you call 911?**
- a) No, that violates privacy
- b) Only if the user gives permission
- c) Yes, under the emergency exception to HIPAA
- d) Only if you get a court order

**Answer:** c

---

**3. How long must you retain audit logs?**
- a) 1 year
- b) 3 years
- c) 6 years
- d) Forever

**Answer:** c

---

**4. A user's mother calls asking about their screening. Can you tell her?**
- a) Yes, she's family
- b) Only if she sounds really worried
- c) No, never without user's permission
- d) Only if she offers to pay

**Answer:** c

---

**5. What is ePHI?**
- a) Electronic Protected Health Information
- b) Emergency Private Health Information
- c) Encrypted Personal Health Information
- d) External Public Health Information

**Answer:** a

---

**6. You find a laptop with PHI in a coffee shop. What do you do?**
- a) Take it home and keep it safe
- b) Report to security officer immediately
- c) Try to find the owner yourself
- d) Wipe it and sell it

**Answer:** b

---

**7. How often must you complete HIPAA training?**
- a) Once when hired
- b) Every 6 months
- c) Annually
- d) Only if there's a breach

**Answer:** c

---

**8. Can you discuss PHI in an elevator?**
- a) Yes, as long as you whisper
- b) No, never
- c) Only if the elevator is empty
- d) Only with colleagues

**Answer:** b

---

**9. What is the penalty for knowingly violating HIPAA?**
- a) Nothing, it's just a guideline
- b) Slap on the wrist
- c) Civil and criminal penalties, fines up to $1.5M, prison time
- d) Just have to take training again

**Answer:** c

---

**10. Who is responsible for HIPAA compliance at PsychSync?**
- a) Just the compliance officer
- b) Just the clinicians
- c) Just management
- d) EVERYONE

**Answer:** d

## 🔄 Ongoing Compliance

### **Annual Refresher Training**

**Schedule:**
- Send reminder 2 weeks before anniversary
- Use same training manual (condensed version available)
- Shorter quiz (5-10 questions)
- Update manual if laws changed

### **New Hires**

**Timeline:** Complete within first week of employment
- Include in onboarding checklist
- Assign buddy/mentor for questions
- Verify before granting system access

### **Policy Changes**

**If HIPAA regulations change:**
- Update training manual within 30 days
- Retrain all affected staff on changes
- Document what changed and why
