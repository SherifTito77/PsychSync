## 🎤 objection Handling Scripts (Top 10)

### 1. "It's too expensive"
**Response:** "PsychSync is $1,800/year for a 10-person team. One turnover costs $45K. We reduce turnover by 50%. That's a 12x return on investment in Year 1. How much are you currently spending on turnover?"

### 2. "We don't have budget"
**Response:** "What's your annual turnover cost? If you have 50 employees and 20% turnover (industry average), that's 10 turnovers × $45K = $450K/year. We can cut that in half for $18K/year. What's the ROI on that?"

### 3. "We need to think about it"
**Response:** "Absolutely. What questions do you still have? Let me address those now so you have everything you need to make a decision this week. Can we schedule a 15-minute follow-up for Tuesday?"

### 4. "I need to talk to my team"
**Response:** "Great! Who else needs to be involved? I can create a demo specifically for [HR/Manager/Executive] so they see the value in 5 minutes. Want me to send that over now?"

### 5. "We use [Competitor]"
**Response:** "[Competitor] is great for [what they do well]. PsychSync is different because we [differentiation - e.g., predict conflicts vs. just describe them]. Would it be worth 15 minutes to see how that could help your team?"

### 6. "This seems like just another assessment tool"
**Response:** "Assessments are just 10% of what we do. The real value is in [actionable insights - conflict prediction, team optimization, manager playbooks]. Would you like to see those features?"

### 7. "My team won't like being assessed"
**Response:** "Totally valid concern. That's why we focus on [team-level insights, not individual judgment]. Plus, 85% of our users say they understand their teammates better after using PsychSync. Want to see how we present this to teams?"

### 8. "I don't have time to implement this"
**Response:** "Most teams are up and running in <5 days. We handle everything: onboarding, training, support. What's your biggest time concern - setup, getting team buy-in, or something else?"

### 9. "Is this scientifically valid?"
**Response:** "Yes! We use the Big Five model, the gold standard in personality research (validated in 10,000+ studies). Plus, we have a PhD psychometrician on staff. Would you like to see our methodology whitepaper?"

### 10. "Can I try before I buy?"
**Response:** "Absolutely! We have a 14-day free trial - full access, no credit card required. Plus, if you complete the trial, we'll apply [10% discount/first month free]. Want me to set that up for you?"

## 🎯 Discovery Call Framework

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Goal:** Qualify opportunity and build rapport

### Opening (5 minutes)
1. Build rapport: "Thanks for taking the time..."
2. Set agenda: "I'd like to learn about your team dynamics and see if PsychSync can help..."
3. Get permission: "Is it okay if I ask some questions about your team?"

### Discovery (15 minutes)
**Questions to Ask:**

**Team Dynamics:**
- "How big is your team?"
- "How would you describe your team culture?"
- "What's the biggest people challenge you're facing right now?"

**Pain Points:**
- "Tell me about the last time you had to deal with a conflict on your team..."
- "How do you currently identify potential issues before they escalate?"
- "What's the cost to your team when people problems aren't addressed early?"

**Impact:**
- "How much time do you spend managing interpersonal issues?"
- "What's the impact on team productivity when conflicts arise?"
- "Have you lost good people because of team fit issues?"

**Current Solutions:**
- "What tools are you currently using to understand team dynamics?"
- "What's working about those tools?"
- "What's missing?"

### Qualification (5 minutes)
- **Budget:** "Do you have budget for team development tools?"
- **Authority:** "Who else would need to approve this?"
- **Need:** "On a scale of 1-10, how important is solving this for you right now?"
- **Timeline:** "When are you looking to implement a solution?"

### Next Steps (5 minutes)
1. **Confirm fit:** "Based on what you've shared, PsychSync sounds like a good fit..."
2. **Propose next step:** "I'd love to show you a 15-minute demo tailored to your team..."
3. **Schedule:** "Are you free [day/time] for that?"
4. **Confirm:** "I'll send a calendar invite with the demo link. Sound good?"
