## Email #1: Request for Organization Policy Quote

**Subject:** Request for Malpractice Insurance Quote - Mental Health Crisis Intervention Platform

**To:** quotes@insurance-provider.com

**Body:**

Dear [Insurance Provider Name],

I'm requesting a quote for professional liability (malpractice) insurance for PsychSync, a mental health crisis intervention platform.

**About Our Organization**

PsychSync provides evidence-based mental health screening with automated crisis detection and intervention. Our platform:

- Offers validated screening tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS)
- Detects suicide risk and alerts on-call crisis clinicians
- Provides 24/7 crisis intervention by licensed mental health professionals
- Handles protected health information (PHI) in HIPAA-compliant manner

**Coverage Needed**

We're seeking a **group practice policy** covering:

1. **Professional Liability** for crisis intervention services
2. **Vicarious Liability** for the organization
3. **General Liability** for business operations
4. **Cyber Liability** for data breaches involving PHI
5. **Licensing Board Defense** endorsement

**Clinicians to be Covered:**

- **3 Crisis Clinicians** (LCSW, LMFT, or LPCC)
  - Providing remote crisis intervention
  - Working from home offices
  - Covering all 50 states (users nationwide)
  - On-call rotation (24/7 coverage)

**Coverage Requirements:**

- **Per Occurrence Limit:** $1,000,000
- **Aggregate Limit:** $3,000,000
- **Consent to Settle:** Required (we must approve any settlements)
- **Telehealth/Remote Services:** Must be explicitly included
- **Crisis Intervention:** Must be explicitly included
- **Policy Type:** Occurrence (preferred) or Claims-Made

**Risk Management Features:**

We have implemented:
- [ ] Comprehensive crisis intervention protocols
- [ ] Annual safety training for all clinicians
- [ ] Regular clinical supervision
- [ ] Quality improvement program
- [ ] HIPAA-compliant data security
- [ ] Comprehensive audit logging
- [ ] Background checks for all clinical staff

**Information Needed:**

To provide an accurate quote, please let us know:

1. **Annual Premium:** Expected cost for $1M/$3M coverage
2. **Deductible Options:** What deductibles are available?
3. **Payment Terms:** Annual, quarterly, or monthly?
4. **Add-On Coverages:**
   - Cyber liability endorsement cost?
   - Licensing board defense cost?
   - Abuse/molestation coverage (if applicable)?
5. **Risk Management Credits:** Do you offer discounts for our safety protocols?
6. **Claims History:** What's your experience with mental health crisis intervention?
7. **Financial Strength:** What is your A.M. Best rating?
8. **Consent to Settle:** Can you add this clause?

**Timeline:**

We're looking to obtain coverage by [date] and would appreciate quotes by [earlier date].

**Documentation Available:**

Upon request, we can provide:
- Complete crisis intervention protocols
- Clinician credentials and licensing information
- Risk management procedures
- HIPAA security policies
- Business background and financials

Thank you for your consideration. We look forward to your quote.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
PsychSync
Email: [your-email@psychsync.ai]
Phone: [your-phone-number]
Website: https://psychsync.ai

## Email #2: Follow-Up Questions After Quote

**Subject:** Questions About Insurance Quote - PsychSync

**To:** quotes@insurance-provider.com

**Body:**

Dear [Insurance Provider Name],

Thank you for providing the quote for PsychSync's malpractice insurance. We have a few questions before making our decision:

**Quote Reference:** [Quote Number or Reference]

**Questions:**

1. **Policy Type:**
   - Is this a Claims-Made or Occurrence policy?
   - If Claims-Made, what is the cost for tail coverage?

2. **Consent to Settle:**
   - Does the policy include a "consent to settle" clause?
   - Can you confirm we must approve any settlements?

3. **Telehealth Coverage:**
   - Are remote/telehealth services explicitly included?
   - Are there any exclusions we should be aware of?

4. **Crisis Intervention:**
   - Is crisis intervention and suicide prevention explicitly covered?
   - Are there any high-risk exclusions?

5. **State Coverage:**
   - Our clinicians are licensed in [states]
   - Our users may be in any of the 50 states
   - Are we covered for interstate practice?

6. **Cyber Liability:**
   - Does the quote include cyber liability?
   - What's the coverage limit for data breaches?
   - Does it cover HIPAA breach response costs?

7. **Adding New Clinicians:**
   - What's the process for adding new clinicians to the policy?
   - Is there additional cost for each new clinician?
   - How much notice is required?

8. **Claims Process:**
   - What is the claims reporting process?
   - What is the response time for claims?
   - Who will be assigned to handle our claims?

9. **Risk Management:**
   - Do you offer risk management resources?
   - Are there discounts for safety training and protocols?
   - Can you provide sample protocols or guidelines?

10. **References:**
    - Can you provide references from other mental health organizations you insure?

**Comparison:**

We're also reviewing quotes from [other providers]. Your response to these questions will help us make our decision.

**Next Steps:**

We're hoping to make a decision by [date]. Your prompt response would be appreciated.

Thank you,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
PsychSync

## ✅ Insurance Checklist

**Before requesting quotes:**
- [ ] Gather clinician information
- [ ] Document risk management features
- [ ] Prepare organization background
- [ ] Determine coverage limits needed
- [ ] Decide: Occurrence vs. Claims-Made policy

**When comparing quotes:**
- [ ] Compare coverage limits
- [ ] Compare deductibles
- [ ] Check for consent to settle clause
- [ ] Verify telehealth is included
- [ ] Verify crisis intervention is included
- [ ] Check cyber liability coverage
- [ ] Compare financial strength (A.M. Best rating)
- [ ] Ask for references

**Before purchasing:**
- [ ] Understand all exclusions
- [ ] Confirm consent to settle clause is included
- [ ] Verify all services are covered
- [ ] Understand claims process
- [ ] Confirm effective date

**After purchasing:**
- [ ] Add all clinicians immediately
- [ ] Provide certificates of insurance to clinicians
- [ ] File policy in safe place
- [ ] Set up calendar for renewal reminder
- [ ] Schedule annual review
