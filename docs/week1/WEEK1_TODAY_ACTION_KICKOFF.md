## 🚨 Common Week 1 Pitfalls to Avoid

### **Pitfall 1: Skipping the Prep Work**
**Don't do this:** "I'll just wing it in the meeting"
**Do this instead:** Spend 1 hour Sunday + 2 hours Monday morning preparing

### **Pitfall 2: Not Documenting Decisions**
**Don't do this:** Making decisions in meetings without writing them down
**Do this instead:** Use the decision log template from Thursday's kit

### **Pitfall 3: No Follow-Through**
**Don't do this:** Great meetings, but nothing happens next week
**Do this instead:** Assign action items with owners and due dates in every meeting

### **Pitfall 4: Skipping the Sales Training**
**Don't do this:** "Sales team will figure it out"
**Do this instead:** Full 4-hour training with roleplay and certification

### **Pitfall 5: No Risk Tracking**
**Don't do this:** "We'll handle risks as they come up"
**Do this instead:** Weekly risk review every Friday at 3:00 PM
