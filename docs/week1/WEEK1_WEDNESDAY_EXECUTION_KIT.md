## 🎯 WEDNESDAY LEARNINGS

**Executive Review Preparation:**
- **Why 2-3 Questions Each?** Questions show you've done the homework and are engaged. They also uncover blind spots in the presentations. Good questions challenge assumptions, probe for risks, and explore trade-offs.
- **Why Pre-Brief Teams?** Your team has insights you don't. Engineering knows if Sprint 1 is realistic. Sales knows if lead gen targets are achievable. CS knows if retention targets are ambitious. Pre-briefing incorporates their input and prevents surprises.
- **Why Rehearse Presentations?** 20 minutes flies by. Rehearsing ensures you hit your key points, stay on time, and deliver a polished presentation. It also helps you anticipate questions and objections.

**Sprint Execution:**
- **Why Clear Blockers First?** Database schema and migrations block everything else. Frontend can't integrate without API design. Data science can't validate without data structure. Clearing blockers unlocks parallel work.
- **Why Track Progress Daily?** Daily standups and sprint updates expose problems early. If BE-103 is blocked, everyone knows. If Sprint 1 slips, we know by Wednesday, not Friday. Daily tracking enables course correction.
- **Why Activation Dashboard?** You can't improve what you don't measure. The activation dashboard reveals friction points (e.g., only 50% of teams reach Day 7 milestone). Fixing these friction points improves retention.
