### Part 3: Objection Handling (11:00 AM-12:00 PM)

**Facilitator:** VP Sales
**Goal:** Teach AEs to handle the 15 most common objections

**Objection 1: "This is too expensive"**

**Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge:** "I understand budget is a concern."
2. **Reframe:** "Have you calculated how much turnover costs you?"
3. **ROI Math:** "For a 50-person team, 40% churn = 20 people leaving/year. At $50K recruiting cost per person, that's $1M/year. PsychSync costs $10.8K/year. That's a 93x ROI."
4. **Case Study:** "TechCo reduced turnover from 35% to 12% in 6 months. They saved $200K in the first year."
5. **Close:** "Ready to see how much you could save?"

**Objection 2: "We don't have time for this"**

**Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge:** "I get it—you're busy."
2. **Time Commitment:** "It takes 10 minutes for your team to complete assessments. That's it."
3. **Immediate Value:** "You'll see your team's personality map tomorrow morning."
4. **Time Savings:** "How much time do you spend dealing with team conflict? Probably hours per week. PsychSync reduces conflict by 15%, saving you 5+ hours/week."
5. **Close:** "Can you spare 10 minutes to save 5 hours/week?"

**Objection 3: "This sounds like hiring bias"**

**Response Framework:**
1. **Clarify:** "Great question—we never use personality for hiring decisions."
2. **Use Case:** "We only analyze teams AFTER people are hired. Think of it as team building, not candidate screening."
3. **Legal Protection:** "We're SOC 2 certified and GDPR-compliant. Individual data is confidential—only aggregated team data is shared."
4. **Anti-Bias:** "In fact, we help REDUCE bias. Managers often have unconscious biases about their team. Our data provides objective insights."
5. **Close:** "Want to see our legal whitepaper?"

**Objection 4: "I need to talk to my team/boss/Legal"**

**Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge:** "Absolutely—this is a team decision."
2. **Equip Them:** "What materials would help? I can send you a one-pager, ROI calculator, or security whitepaper."
3. **Offer to Present:** "Happy to do a 15-minute presentation to your team/boss. I'll tailor it to your specific use case."
4. **Trial First:** "Or we can start with a free trial. Once your team sees the data, they'll be convinced."
5. **Follow-Up:** "When should I follow up? Tuesday or Wednesday?"

**Objection 5: "We already do engagement surveys/360 reviews/annual reviews"**

**Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge:** "Those are great tools—but they're different."
2. **Differentiate:** "Engagement surveys tell you how people FEEL (retrospective). PsychSync tells you how people BEHAVE (predictive)."
3. **Analogy:** "Surveys are like checking the weather after it rains. PsychSync is like a weather forecast—we predict conflicts BEFORE they happen."
4. **Complementary:** "PsychSync doesn't replace surveys—it complements them. Use surveys for culture, PsychSync for team dynamics."
5. **Close:** "Want to see how PsychSync predicts what surveys miss?"

**Backend (`app/core/analytics.py`):**
```python
import mixpanel
from app.core.config import settings

mp = mixpanel.Mixpanel(settings.MIXPANEL_TOKEN)

def track_event(user_id: str, event_name: str, properties: dict = None):
    """Track event in Mixpanel"""
    if properties is None:
        properties = {}

    # Add common properties
    properties.update({
        "time": int(datetime.now().timestamp()),
        "os": "backend",
        "app_version": settings.VERSION,
    })

    mp.track(user_id, event_name, properties)

def track_user_profile(user_id: str, properties: dict):
    """Set user properties in Mixpanel"""
    mp.people_set(user_id, properties)
```

## 🎯 TUESDAY LEARNINGS

**Sales Training Insights:**
- **Why 4 Hours?** Sales training isn't just about memorizing scripts—it's about internalizing the PsychSync story so deeply that it becomes natural. The 4-hour format includes role-play and objection handling practice, which builds muscle memory.
- **Why Certification?** Certification ensures AEs can actually sell, not just attend. The 80% bar ensures mastery without being unrealistic. Those who don't pass get a chance to retake on Friday.
- **Why Role-Specific Pitches?** Different buyers have different pain points. VP HR cares about turnover costs. Founders care about speed. People Leaders care about scalability. One-size-fits-all pitches don't work.

**Engineering Setup Insights:**
- **Why Linear over Jira?** Linear is faster and more intuitive for small teams. Jira is more powerful but has a steeper learning curve. Start with Linear, migrate to Jira if you outgrow it.
- **Why 22 Tasks?** Sprint 1 has 22 tasks (78 story points) because we're building a complete feature (backend + frontend + data science + QA). Breaking it down into small tasks enables parallel execution and accurate progress tracking.
- **Why Automations?** Automations reduce administrative overhead. Auto-assigning issues to sprint, notifying reviewers when PRs are ready, and escalating blocked issues keeps the team focused on shipping code, not managing Jira.

**Analytics Setup Insights:**
- **Why Both Mixpanel and Amplitude?** Mixpanel is better for product analytics (funnels, retention, cohorts). Amplitude is better for user analytics (DAU/MAU, session length). Use both to get a complete picture.
- **Why 50+ Events?** Granular event tracking enables deep analysis. Don't just track "app opened"—track "team personality map viewed," "AI insights viewed," "manager playbook accessed." This reveals which features drive engagement.
- **Why 5 Critical Dashboards?** Dashboards align the team on North Star metrics. Everyone should know: How's our 90-day retention? How many teams are activating? What's our DAU/MAU? These metrics prevent feature factory syndrome (building features nobody uses).
