## 🎯 FRIDAY LEARNINGS

**All-Hands Insights:**
- **Why Present Vision to Everyone?** Transparency builds trust. When everyone knows the 2-year vision, they understand how their work contributes. A QA tester fixing bugs may feel disconnected—but if they know those bugs prevent churn reduction, which prevents $50M ARR, they feel connected to the mission.
- **Why Q&A?** Questions reveal gaps in understanding. If 5 people ask "Is this realistic?", maybe the vision is too aggressive. If 5 people ask "What's my role?", maybe we haven't communicated role-specific expectations clearly enough.
- **Why Celebrate Week 1?** Momentum is everything. If we don't celebrate Week 1, the team feels like "we worked hard but nobody noticed." Celebration reinforces the effort and builds energy for Week 2.

**Risk Review Insights:**

- **Why QA Test Results?** Test results reveal quality. 82% coverage is great. 0 critical bugs is great. But 2 medium bugs (UI polish) reminds us we're not done yet. Quality is a journey, not a destination.
