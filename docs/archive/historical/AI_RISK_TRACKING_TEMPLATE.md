## Appendix: Quick Reference

### Risk Scoring Guide

```
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Likelihood:
- Low = 1-2
- Medium = 3
- High = 4-5

Impact (1-5):
- 1: Minimal
- 2: Minor
- 3: Moderate
- 4: Major
- 5: Critical

Risk Levels:
- Critical: 20-25 (P0)
- High: 15-19 (P1)
- Medium: 9-14 (P2)
- Low: 1-8 (P3)
```

### Status Definitions

- **Open**: Risk identified, mitigation not started
- **In Progress**: Mitigation underway
- **Blocked**: Mitigation blocked by dependency
- **Closed**: Risk fully mitigated
- **Accepted**: Risk accepted (no mitigation planned)

### NIST AI RMF Functions

- **Govern**: Establish governance, policies, accountability
- **Map**: Contextualize, analyze, categorize risks
- **Measure**: Implement metrics, monitoring, testing
- **Manage**: Prioritize, implement, monitor, update controls
