### 3. Discovery Call Script

#### **Opening (2 minutes)**
```
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time to speak with me today. Before we dive in, could you tell me a bit about your role and what team challenges you're currently facing at [Company Name]?"

"Great. So I understand you're dealing with [Their Challenge]. That's actually one of the most common issues we help companies solve."
```

#### **Problem Exploration (8 minutes)**
```
**Key Questions:**
- "How are you currently assessing team performance and collaboration?"
- "What tools or methods are you using for personality assessments?"
- "How do you measure team effectiveness and productivity?"
- "What's the biggest challenge when building new teams or integrating team members?"
- "How do you identify and resolve team communication issues?"
- "What's the impact when teams aren't working optimally?"

**Pain Point Probing:**
- "How much time is lost to miscommunication or team conflicts?"
- "What happens when team members' working styles don't align?"
- "How do you currently onboard new team members?"
- "What metrics do you use to evaluate team success?"
```

1. **Multi-Framework Integration:** Show how MBTI + Big Five + DISC + Enneagram + Predictive Index + StrengthsFinder work together
2. **Real-Time Team Analytics:** Demonstrate live dashboard with team insights
3. **Communication Pattern Analysis:** Show how different personality types interact

**Live Demo Flow:**
- Show sample team dashboard with real data

1. **Team-Level Insights:** Individual assessments don't show how teams work together
2. **Communication Patterns:** Traditional tools miss team dynamics and conflicts
3. **Real-Time Analysis:** Static reports can't adapt to team changes
4. **Integration Capabilities:** Your current tool likely lacks HRIS integration

#### **Competitive Battle Cards**
```
Against SHL:
- **Advantage:** 6 frameworks vs. 1, AI insights vs. static reports, team focus vs. individual
- **Pricing:** 30-50% lower total cost of ownership
- **Weakness:** Less brand recognition, fewer large enterprise references
- **Response:** Emphasize innovation and ROI, offer pilot program

Against Culture Amp:
- **Advantage:** Deep assessment insights vs. engagement surveys, predictive analytics vs. retrospective
- **Functionality:** Team optimization vs. measurement only
- **Weakness:** Smaller customer base, less established market presence
- **Response:** Focus on scientific validation and predictive capabilities

Against Lattice:
- **Advantage:** Scientific assessment rigor vs. qualitative feedback, team focus vs. individual
- **Integration:** Assessment + analytics vs. feedback only
- **Weakness:** Less established brand recognition
- **Response:** Highlight multi-framework benefits and ROI
```
