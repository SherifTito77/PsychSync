## Anticipated Board Questions & Answers

**Q1: Why won't Gallup or 15Five just build this?**

A: "They could, but three things stop them. First, data - we have 10,000 teams of data that would take them 18-24 months to collect. Second, product focus - Gallup is individual assessments, not teams; 15Five is surveys, not science. Both would need to rebuild their product from scratch. Third, culture - we're product-led and engineering-first; they're sales-led and consultancy-first. Innovation velocity favors us."

**Q2: What if your ML models aren't accurate enough?**

A: "We're already at 78% accuracy with 10,000 teams. Our models improve every week as we add more data. We're also transparent about confidence intervals - we show users '78% ±5%' so they know the uncertainty. And we have human oversight - low-confidence predictions are flagged for review. But most importantly, even at 78% accuracy, we're providing value managers don't have today. An early warning is better than no warning."

**Q3: How do you acquire customers efficiently?**
