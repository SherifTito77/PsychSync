### Executive Vision & Strategy

#### **2_YEAR_PRODUCT_VISION.md**
**Audience:** CEO, CPO, All Executives, Board
**Purpose:** Define 2-year trajectory to $50M ARR (2025-2027)
**Key Sections:**
- 2025: $0 → $5M ARR (500 → 30,000 teams)
- 2026: $5M → $20M ARR (30K → 70K teams)
- 2027: $20M → $50M ARR (70K → 100K teams)
- 6 strategic bets (Team Analytics, Conflict Prediction, AI Coach, etc.)
- Feature sequencing by quarter
- Competitive moats (network effects, data assets, switching costs)
**Use When:** Understanding long-term direction, making roadmap trade-offs, presenting to board

#### **COMPETITIVE_ADVANTAGES.md**
**Audience:** CPO, CEO, VP Sales, Marketing
**Purpose:** PsychSync's 6 defensible competitive advantages
**Key Sections:**
- Network Effects (team-based assessments compound value)
- Data Assets (proprietary personality → outcome dataset)
- Switching Costs (historical data, benchmarking, integrations)
- Regulatory Moats (SOC 2, GDPR, scientific validity)
- Product differentiation (Big Five vs. competitors)
- Brand & Community (psychological science thought leadership)
**Use When:** Answering "Why PsychSync vs. competitors?", investor questions, positioning statements

#### **PRODUCT_DIFFERENTIATION_STRATEGY.md**
**Audience:** CPO, VP Sales, Marketing, Product Team
**Purpose:** Head-to-head competitive positioning vs. 8 competitors
**Key Sections:**
- vs. Gallup (strengths-based): We predict outcomes, not just describe
- vs. BetterUp (coaching): 1/10th price, scalable to 1000-employee teams
- vs. Culture Amp (surveys): Personality-based predictions, not retrospective surveys
- vs. Myers-Briggs (legacy): Scientific validity (Big Five vs. unscientific MBTI)
- Feature differentiation matrix
**Use When:** Sales calls, product roadmap debates, marketing copywriting

#### **PRODUCT_RISK_ANALYSIS.md**
**Audience:** CEO, Head of Data Science, General Counsel, CPO
**Purpose:** Identify and mitigate 4 critical product risks
**Key Sections:**
- Risk 1: Scientific Validity (mitigation: hire PhD psychometrician, publish whitepapers)
- Risk 2: Data Breach (mitigation: encryption at rest, penetration testing, audit logging)
- Risk 3: ML Model Accuracy (mitigation: confidence intervals, retraining pipeline)
- Risk 4: Regulatory Compliance (mitigation: geofencing, bias audits, legal review)
**Use When:** Board meetings, investor due diligence, hiring decisions, security audits
