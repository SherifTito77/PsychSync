# In-App Onboarding Microcopy
# PsychSync User Onboarding Copy Library

## Overview

This document provides production-ready microcopy for PsychSync's user onboarding experience. All copy is designed to be clear, concise, and conversion-optimized while maintaining a friendly, professional tone.

**Copy Principles:**
- ✅ Action-oriented (tells users what to do)
- ✅ Benefit-focused (explains "why")
- ✅ Concise (5-7 words for buttons)
- ✅ Conversational (natural language)
- ✅ Accessible (reading level: 8th grade)
- ✅ Inclusive (gender-neutral pronouns)

## Writing Guidelines

### Voice & Tone

**Our brand voice is:**
- **Friendly but professional** – like a knowledgeable colleague
- **Clear and concise** – respect users' time
- **Encouraging, not pushy** – guide, don't demand
- **Human, not robotic** – use natural language

**Do:**
- ✅ Use "you" and "your" (direct address)
- ✅ Write in active voice
- ✅ Use simple words (readability: 8th grade level)
- ✅ Be specific and concrete
- ✅ Show empathy

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use jargon or buzzwords
- ❌ Write in passive voice
- ❌ Be vague or ambiguous
- ❌ Use exclamation points excessively
- ❌ Sound robotic or formal

---

### Button Copy Standards

**Best practices:**
- 5-7 words maximum
- Start with verb
- Be specific about what happens
- Use title case

**Good examples:**
- ✅ "View My Results"
- ✅ "Start Assessment"
- ✅ "Upgrade to Premium"
- ✅ "Invite Team Members"

**Bad examples:**
- ❌ "Submit" (too vague)
- ❌ "Click here" (unprofessional)
- ❌ "Proceed with the generation of your assessment report" (too long)
- ❌ "Okay" (not descriptive)

### Accessibility Considerations

**WCAG 2.1 compliance:**

**Color contrast:**
- All text: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Large text (18pt+): Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio
- Interactive elements: Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio

**Screen reader support:**
- All images have alt text
- Form fields have labels
- Error messages are announced
- Buttons have descriptive text (not just "click here")

**Language:**
- Plain language (8th grade reading level)
- Avoid idioms and metaphors
- Explain technical terms
- Provide context for actions
