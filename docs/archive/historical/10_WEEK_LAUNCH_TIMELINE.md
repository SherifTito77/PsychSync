## 🚨 Risk Management

### **Potential Delays & Mitigation**

**Risk:** Difficulty hiring clinicians
**Mitigation:** Increase compensation, use recruiting agency, expand search

**Risk:** Legal review takes longer
**Mitigation:** Start earlier, budget for expedited review, be flexible on timeline

**Risk:** Insurance costs higher than expected
**Mitigation:** Get multiple quotes, adjust coverage limits, explore different providers

**Risk:** Technical issues discovered during testing
**Mitigation:** Extended testing period, buffer week before launch

**Risk:** Clinician burnout before launch
**Mitigation:** Hire backup clinicians, keep on-call light during training
