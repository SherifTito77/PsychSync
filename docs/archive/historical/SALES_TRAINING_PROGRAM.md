## Part 1: The PsychSync Story (60 Minutes)

### The Core Narrative Framework

**The HERO Story Structure:**

```
H - Hook (Opening)
  → "What if I told you your team is spending 40% of their time on
     avoidable conflict?"

E - Empathy (Validate Pain)
  → "I know how frustrating that is. Sarah Chen, an engineering
     manager I worked with, spent 3 hours every week mediating
     personality clashes. Nothing she tried worked."

R - Reveal (The Insight)
  → "Here's what was actually happening: Sarah's team wasn't broken.
     They just weren't optimized. Sarah is an INTJ (needs time to
     think, hates surprises). Marcus is an ENFP (thinks out loud,
     craves variety). They weren't being difficult - just different."

O - Offer (The Solution)
  → "PsychSync shows you your team's personality landscape - literally.
     We map every team member's communication style, decision-making
     preferences, energy sources, and stress triggers. Then we do
     something no one else does: WE PREDICT PROBLEMS BEFORE THEY HAPPEN."

O - Outcome (The Result)
  → "Three months later? Sarah's conflict time dropped from 40% to
     10%. Team velocity went up 35%. Her top developer who was
     about to quit? Got promoted instead. PsychSync gave Sarah X-ray
     vision into her team."
```

### Exercise: Tell Your Story (15 Minutes)

**Instructions:**
1. Pair up (AE pairs)
2. Each person tells the PsychSync story using HERO framework
3. Partner provides feedback (what worked, what didn't)
4. Switch roles

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- [ ] Included a compelling hook (opened with a question or bold claim)
- [ ] Validated the prospect's pain (empathy, not just facts)
- [ ] Revealed the insight (the "aha moment")
- [ ] Presented PsychSync as the solution (differentiated)
- [ ] Quantified the outcome (specific metrics)

## Part 3: Objection Handling Workshop (60 Minutes)

### The "Feel, Felt, Found" Framework

```
1. FEEL (Validate)
   → "I completely understand your concern. Many team leads I work
      with felt the same way at first."

2. FELT (Share Similar Experience)
   → "Sarah Chen, an engineering manager, was worried her team would
      see PsychSync as 'just another personality test.' She thought
      they'd resist."

3. FOUND (Share Resolution)
   → "But what she found was: When she framed it as 'This is about me
      learning to manage you better,' not 'You're being judged,' her
      team embraced it. One team member even said: 'Finally, a manager
      who gets us!'"
```

>
> Would you be open to trying it with 3-5 team members first, no pressure?"

**Script:**
> "Totally get it. Budgets are tight.
>
> But let me ask: What are you spending on turnover?
>

> - Recruiting: $15K
> - Onboarding: $10K
> - Lost productivity: $20K

>

>
> Here's the thing: Gallup is fantastic for individuals. 15Five is excellent for engagement surveys. But neither of them answers the question: 'How do I MANAGE my team day-to-day?'
>
> Gallup gives Sarah a report: 'You're an INTJ.' She reads it, thinks 'interesting,' puts it in a drawer. Never looks at it again.
>

>

> - 'When you present to Marcus (ENFP), send him an agenda 24 hours in advance.'
> - 'Your team is 80% Introverted. Stop scheduling spontaneous meetings.'
> - 'You and Jessica have 62% communication compatibility. Here's how to optimize.'

>
