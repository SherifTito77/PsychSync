## Executive Summary

This document assigns **executive ownership** for PsychSync's 4 critical risks (Score 80-125) and establishes a **risk management framework** for ongoing mitigation.

**Key Principle:** "What gets measured gets managed. What gets owned gets done."

**Critical Success Factors:**
1. **Single Owner:** Each risk has ONE executive owner (not shared responsibility)
2. **Authority:** Owners have budget and decision-making authority to mitigate risks
3. **Accountability:** Owners report progress weekly to CEO/executive team
4. **Resources:** Each risk has dedicated budget and team support

**Risk Statement:**

## Part 2: Risk Management Process

### Weekly Risk Review (Fridays 3pm, 30 minutes)

**Attendees:** CEO + 4 Risk Owners + CPO + VP CS

**Agenda:**
1. **Status Updates** (5 min per risk, 20 min total)
   - Each owner shares progress (2 min)
   - Report metrics/roadblocks (3 min)

2. **New Risks** (5 min)
   - Any new risks identified this week?
   - Add to Risk Register if score >50

3. **Escalations** (5 min)
   - Any risks need CEO/board attention?
   - Any risks need additional resources?

**Output:**
- Updated Risk Register (all 26 risks, current scores)
- Action items (owner + deadline)
- Escalation email (if needed)

---

### Quarterly Risk Audit (Last Friday of Quarter)

**Attendees:** All executives + department heads

**Activities:**
1. Re-score all risks (probability, impact, velocity may have changed)
2. Identify emerging risks (new technologies, regulations, competitors)
3. Retire mitigated risks (score <25)
4. Prioritize next quarter's focus (top 3 risks)
5. Update Risk Register (publish to company wiki)

**Output:**
- Quarterly Risk Report (5-page executive summary)
- Updated Risk Priorities (top 3 risks for next quarter)
- Risk Budget for next quarter (allocate resources)
