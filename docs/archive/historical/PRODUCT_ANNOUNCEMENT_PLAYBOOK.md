# Product Announcement Playbook
**Strategic Framework for Launching Features at PsychSync**

**Date:** January 12, 2025
**Status:** Active Framework
**Audience:** Product Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, Engineering, Leadership

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This playbook ensures every product announcement:
1. **Drives adoption** - Gets users excited and activates them quickly
2. **Supports sales** - Gives AEs talking points to close deals
3. **Reduces churn** - Shows existing customers we're innovating
4. **Builds brand** - Positions PsychSync as the thought leader in team intelligence

**Core Principles:**
- **Customer-first** - Lead with value, not features
- **Multi-channel** - Meet customers where they are (email, in-app, social, sales)
- **Segmented** - Tailor messaging to different user personas
- **Measured** - Track adoption, engagement, and business impact

---

## 📊 Announcement Tiers

Not all features are created equal. Match your announcement effort to feature impact.

### Tier 1: Major Releases (Quarterly)
**Examples:** New assessment framework, Team Personality Map, Slack Integration, Conflict Early-Warning

**Impact:** Drives 10%+ ARR growth or 15%+ retention improvement

**Announcement Investment:** $25K+ budget, 3-week campaign

### Tier 2: Feature Releases (Monthly)
**Examples:** Manager Playbooks, Team Comparison View, Historical Trends, Custom Reports

**Impact:** Drives 3-5% ARR growth or 5-10% engagement improvement

**Announcement Investment:** $5-10K budget, 1-week campaign

### Tier 3: Enhancements & Fixes (Weekly)
**Examples:** UX improvements, bug fixes, small feature additions

**Impact:** Quality of life improvements, reduces support tickets

**Announcement Investment:** Minimal, in-app + release notes only

### Tier 4: Silent Updates (Continuous)
**Examples:** Backend optimizations, performance improvements, security patches

**Impact:** No user-facing changes, technical debt reduction

**Announcement Investment:** None (documented in changelog for internal reference)

## 🚀 Tier 1 Launch Framework (Major Releases)

### Pre-Launch: T-minus 3 Weeks

**Week 3: Positioning & Messaging**
- [ ] **Product Marketing:** Write positioning statement
- [ ] **Product Marketing:** Create customer value proposition (CVP)
- [ ] **Product:** Finalize feature name, description, use cases
- [ ] **Design:** Create launch visuals (screenshots, GIFs, videos)
- [ ] **Content:** Write announcement blog post outline

**Week 2: Asset Creation**
- [ ] **Design:** Finalize all visual assets (social cards, email graphics, in-app banners)
- [ ] **Content:** Write blog post (1,500-2,000 words)
- [ ] **Content:** Write press release (if media-worthy)
- [ ] **Sales:** Create sales battle card (feature talking points, objections, ROI)
- [ ] **CS:** Create customer-facing FAQ (10-15 Q&A)
- [ ] **CS:** Create in-app onboarding flow for new feature
- [ ] **Engineering:** Record demo video (2-3 minutes, voice-over)

**Week 1: Internal Enablement**
- [ ] **Product:** Conduct internal demo (all-hands, 30 minutes)
- [ ] **Sales:** Conduct sales training (battle card review, role-plays)
- [ ] **CS:** Conduct CS training (support troubleshooting, customer comms)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Distribute press release to media (if applicable)
- [ ] **Leadership:** Social proof prep (exec LinkedIn posts planned)

**Launch Readiness Checklist:**
- [ ] Feature is bug-free and QA-approved
- [ ] Documentation is complete (help center articles)
- [ ] Support team is trained (can handle Tier 1 questions)
- [ ] Sales team is trained (can demo feature confidently)
- [ ] All assets are finalized and approved
- [ ] Launch date is communicated internally

---

### Launch Day: T-Minus 0

**6:00 AM PT (Early Morning)**
- [ ] **Engineering:** Deploy feature to production (if not already in dark launch)
- [ ] **Engineering:** Verify all systems operational (no critical bugs)
- [ ] **Product:** Smoke test feature (end-to-end user journey)

**7:00 AM PT (Morning)**
- [ ] **CS:** Enable in-app announcement banner (all users)
- [ ] **CS:** Enable in-product tooltip/first-run experience
- [ ] **CS:** Publish help center documentation
- [ ] **Product:** Verify feature is discoverable in UI

**8:00 AM PT (West Coast Work Hours)**
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish blog post
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish LinkedIn post (company page)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish Twitter/X thread (5-7 tweets)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish Facebook post (if applicable)
- [ ] **Leadership:** CEO/CTO/CPO post on LinkedIn (personal accounts)

**9:00 AM PT (Sales Hours Open)**
- [ ] **Sales:** AEs begin proactive outreach to prospects (feature announcement)
- [ ] **Sales:** AEs begin outbound calls to closed-lost opportunities (re-engage)
- [ ] **CS:** CSMs begin proactive outreach to key accounts (feature announcement)

**10:00 AM PT (Email Blast)**
- [ ] **Marketing:** Send product announcement email (entire customer base)
- [ ] **CS:** Send segmented email to high-value customers (personal touch)

**All Day (Social Media Amplification)**
- [ ] **Marketing:** Engage with comments/questions on all posts
- [ ] **All Employees:** Amplify launch posts (like, comment, share)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Monitor social sentiment (respond to all mentions)

**5:00 PM PT (End of Day)**
- [ ] **Product:** Review launch day metrics (adoption, engagement, bugs)
- [ ] **CS:** Review support tickets (any critical issues?)
- [ ] **Engineering:** On-call for hotfixes (if needed)

---

### Post-Launch: T-plus 1 Week

**Day 2-3: Follow-Up & Amplification**
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish customer testimonial (if early adopter success)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Publish "How It Works" video explainer
- [ ] **Sales:** Conduct prospect webinars (feature demo + Q&A)
- [ ] **CS:** Conduct customer webinars (training + Q&A)

**Day 4-5: Adoption Push**
- [ ] **CS:** Targeted outreach to non-adopters (email, in-app nudge)
- [ ] **Sales:** Feature-focused outreach to active opportunities
- [ ] **Marketing:** Retargeting ads (feature announcement to website visitors)

**Day 6-7: Launch Review**
- [ ] **Product:** Compile launch metrics (adoption, engagement, NPS, revenue impact)
- [ ] **Marketing:** Review channel performance (which drove most traffic?)
- [ ] **Sales:** Review sales impact (demos booked, deals influenced)
- [ ] **CS:** Review customer feedback (positive, negative, feature requests)
- [ ] **All:** Conduct launch retrospective (what worked, what didn't, learnings)

**Launch Success Criteria:**
- [ ] 40%+ user adoption within 7 days
- [ ] 25%+ weekly active users of new feature
- [ ] 15%+ increase in product-qualified leads (PQLs)
- [ ] 10%+ reduction in support tickets (related to problem feature solves)
- [ ] Positive NPS for feature (>40)

## 📊 Launch Metrics Dashboard

### Track These Metrics for Every Launch

**Acquisition Metrics:**
- Email open rate: Target >30%
- Email click-through rate: Target >5%
- Blog post views: Target >1,000 (Tier 1), >500 (Tier 2)
- Social media engagement: Target >500 likes/shares (Tier 1)
- Website traffic spike: Target >50% increase day-of-launch

**Adoption Metrics:**
- Day 1 adoption: Target >10% of users
- Day 7 adoption: Target >40% (Tier 1), >25% (Tier 2)
- Day 30 adoption: Target >60% (Tier 1), >40% (Tier 2)
- Weekly active users: Target >25% (Tier 1), >15% (Tier 2)

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Feature session duration: Target >5 minutes
- Feature repeat usage: Target >3 sessions/user/week
- Feature completion rate: Target >70% (users who complete core action)
- Feature NPS: Target >40

**Business Impact Metrics:**
- Product-qualified leads (PQLs): Target >15% increase
- Sales demos booked: Target >10% increase
- Deals influenced: Target >5 closed deals from feature
- Expansion revenue: Target >10% upsells from feature
- Churn reduction: Target >2 percentage point reduction

**Support Metrics:**
- Support tickets related to feature: Target <5% of adopters
- Average resolution time: Target <24 hours
- Bug reports: Target 0 critical, <3 high

**Sentiment Metrics:**
- Social media sentiment: Target >80% positive
- Customer feedback sentiment: Target >75% positive
- Press mentions: Target >5 (Tier 1 only)

## 🎯 Persona-Based Announcement Strategy

### Team Lead Persona (Primary Decision Maker)
**Messaging Focus:** Team performance, retention, conflict reduction
**Channels:** Email, LinkedIn, In-app, Sales outreach
**Timing:** Tuesday-Thursday mornings (9-11 AM)
**Key Hooks:**
- "Reduce team turnover by 50%"
- "Predict conflicts before they happen"
- "Build a high-performing team culture"

### HRBP Persona (Influencer / Champion)
**Messaging Focus:** Talent development, culture, employee engagement
**Channels:** Email, HR community forums, In-app, CS outreach
**Timing:** Wednesday-Friday mid-day (11 AM - 2 PM)
**Key Hooks:**
- "Data-backed talent development"
- "Scale HR impact with AI"
- "Support every manager with personalized insights"

### C-Suite Persona (Budget Approver)
**Messaging Focus:** ROI, business outcomes, competitive advantage
**Channels:** LinkedIn, CEO/Board referrals, Industry publications
**Timing:** Early morning (6-8 AM) or evening (6-8 PM)
**Key Hooks:**
- "$22K savings per retained employee"
- "70% of teams fail due to people issues — we fix this"
- "Operating system for team intelligence"

### Individual Contributor Persona (End User)
**Messaging Focus:** Self-awareness, career growth, team belonging
**Channels:** In-app, Email, Slack/Teams notifications
**Timing:** During work hours (10 AM - 3 PM)
**Key Hooks:**
- "Understand your work style"
- "Find your place on the team"
- "Grow your career with personalized insights"

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`

**Launch Velocity vs. Launch Quality:** There's a tension between shipping fast and launching well. Tier 1 launches (major features) deserve 3 weeks of prep and multi-channel coordination. Tier 3 launches (small enhancements) should ship continuously with minimal friction. The key is matching announcement effort to feature impact.

**Channel Segmentation:** Not every feature needs a blog post. Email + in-app is 80% of launch effectiveness for most features. Reserve Tier 1 campaigns (blog, press, social amplification) for features that move the needle on ARR or retention. This prevents "announcement fatigue" among your customers.

**Sales-CS Alignment:** The biggest missed opportunity in launches is sales-CS misalignment. Sales announces to prospects before CS tells customers (or vice versa). Fix this by coordinating all outbound communication in a single launch day run sheet, with timed ownership for every channel.
