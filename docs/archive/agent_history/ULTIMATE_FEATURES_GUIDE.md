## 📈 Advanced KPIs Explained

### **1. Top 3 Performers**
**Formula**: Average of all Big Five trait scores
**Use Case**: Recognition, bonuses, promotions
**Example**: John (91.0) > Admin (88.0) > Jane (84.4)

### **2. Retention Risk Alert**
**Formula**: Based on neuroticism score (higher = riskier)
**Use Case**: Proactive retention interventions
**Threshold**: >60% = high risk, 40-60% = moderate, <40% = low

### **3. Diversity Score**
**Formula**: (Unique personality types ÷ Total employees) × 100
**Use Case**: Track cognitive diversity, avoid groupthink
**Target**: >70% = good diversity

### **4. Big Five Averages**
**What It Shows**: Organizational culture profile
**Use Cases**:
- Hiring: Match new hires to culture
- Training: Address org-wide gaps
- Teams: Balance personality types

### **5. Department Performance**
**Metric**: Average conscientiousness per department
**Use Case**: Compare department effectiveness
**Insight**: Higher = more reliable, productive
