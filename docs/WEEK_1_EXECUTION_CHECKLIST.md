`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`

**Week 1 is the most critical week** - it sets the rhythm for the entire quarter. If we get Week 1 right (all teams operational, all rituals in place), Weeks 2-12 will flow smoothly. If we skip Week 1 setup, we'll be playing catch-up for the rest of the quarter.

**The 5 Executive Decisions** are the most important moments of the week. These decisions unlock budget, headcount, and targets. Without these decisions, teams are blocked. With them, everyone is empowered to execute. That's why Executive Review is the can't-miss meeting of the week.

**Rituals > Results** - This Week 1: The results of Week 1 aren't the KPIs we hit (it's too early for that). The results are the **rituals we establish**: daily standups, weekly reviews, sprint planning, risk tracking. These rituals will compound over 12 weeks to deliver the Q1 targets. Focus on building the machine this week, not just shipping code.
