## 🎯 Pricing Philosophy

Our pricing strategy is built on three principles:

1. **Value-Based Alignment:** Price to the ROI we deliver, not to competitor benchmarks
2. **Land and Expand:** Make it easy to start, compelling to upgrade
3. **Sustainable Unit Economics:** Ensure 3x+ LTV/CAC at every tier

**The Math:**
- **Turnover Cost:** $45K per employee (industry average)
- **PsychSync Impact:** 50% reduction in turnover = $22.5K savings per employee
- **PsychSync Price:** $180/user/year = **125x ROI**

**We are underpriced for the value we deliver.** This is intentional for market penetration, but we have significant room to increase pricing as we prove value.

### Enterprise Pricing Model

#### Per-User Pricing (Most Common)
- **$20-25/user/month** (500-1,000 users)
- **$25-30/user/month** (1,000-2,500 users)
- **$30-40/user/month** (2,500+ users, premium for customization)

#### Platform Fee (Alternative Model)
- **$25K/year platform fee** + **$15/user/month**
- **Best for:** Very large deployments (5,000+ users) where per-user economics are lower

#### Enterprise Success Fee (Outcome-Based Pricing - Future)
- **Base fee:** $15/user/month
- **Success fee:** 10% of turnover savings (measured and verified)
- **Example:** 1,000-user company with 20% turnover reduction
  - Savings: 100 employees × $45K × 20% = $900K savings
  - Base fee: 1,000 × $15 × 12 = $180K/year
  - Success fee: 10% × $900K = $90K
  - **Total: $270K/year** (still 3.3x ROI for customer)

## 🎯 Pricing Psychology & Behavioral Economics

### Anchor Pricing
Show **Enterprise** as "anchor" to make **Team** look like a great deal:

```
Bad Example (No anchor):
Starter: $12/user/month
Team: $18/user/month

Good Example (With anchor):
Starter: $12/user/month
Team: $18/user/month ← You save 33% vs. Enterprise!
Enterprise: Custom (typically $30+/user/month)
```

### Decoy Effect (Price as Qualitative Signal)

Add **Professional** tier as decoy to drive upgrades to **Enterprise**:

```
Starter: $12/user/month (up to 20 users)
Team: $18/user/month (up to 100 users)
Professional: $25/user/month (up to 500 users) ← DECOY
Enterprise: Custom (100+ users)

Why Professional Works:
- Makes Enterprise look like a better deal (more features for similar price)
- Creates a clear upgrade path for 100-500 user companies
- Anchors Enterprise pricing as "premium" but "worth it"
```

### Good/Better/Best Positioning

Position **Team** as "best value" to drive middle-tier adoption:

```
Starter (Good)
  For: Small teams just getting started
  Price: $12/user/month
  Focus: Self-service, low friction

Team (Better) ⭐ BEST VALUE
  For: Growing companies scaling culture
  Price: $18/user/month
  Focus: Advanced analytics, integrations, playbooks
  Badge: "Most Popular" (highlight this tier)

Enterprise (Best)
  For: Large organizations with complex needs
  Price: Custom ($20-40/user/month)
  Focus: Security, customization, dedicated support
```

## 💰 Unit Economics & Profitability

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Starter Tier (Self-Serve):**
- Marketing spend: $10K/month (content, SEO, ads)
- Leads generated: 500/month
- Lead-to-customer conversion: 5%
- Customers acquired: 25/month
- **CAC: $400/customer**

**Team Tier (Sales-Assist):**
- Marketing spend: $20K/month
- Sales team (2 AEs): $25K/month
- Leads generated: 200/month
- Lead-to-customer conversion: 10%
- Customers acquired: 20/month
- **CAC: $2,250/customer**

**Enterprise Tier (Sales-Led):**
- Sales team (4 AEs): $50K/month
- Marketing spend: $30K/month
- Leads generated: 50/month
- Lead-to-customer conversion: 20%
- Customers acquired: 10/month
- **CAC: $8,000/customer**

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Assumptions:**
- Average customer lifespan: 3 years (36 months)
- Churn rate: 20% annually (improves to 12% in 2025)
- Expansion revenue: 20% annual upsell/cross-sell

**Starter Tier LTV:**
- Average deal size: $144/year (10 users × $12/user/month × 12 months)
- 3-year value: $432
- Expansion (10% user growth + 1 tier upgrade per 10 customers): +$150
- **LTV: ~$580**

**Team Tier LTV:**
- Average deal size: $2,160/year (50 users × $18/user/month × 12 months)
- 3-year value: $6,480
- Expansion (15% user growth + 1 Enterprise upgrade per 10 customers): +$2,500
- **LTV: ~$9,000**

**Enterprise Tier LTV:**
- Average deal size: $30K/year (500 users × $25/user/month × 12 months)
- 3-year value: $90K
- Expansion (20% user growth + multi-year renewals): +$40K
- **LTV: ~$130K**

---

### LTV:CAC Ratios

**Starter Tier:**
- LTV: $580
- CAC: $400
- **LTV:CAC: 1.4x** ⚠️ Below target (need to reduce CAC or increase pricing)

**Team Tier:**
- LTV: $9,000
- CAC: $2,250
- **LTV:CAC: 4.0x** ✅ Healthy unit economics

**Enterprise Tier:**
- LTV: $130K
- CAC: $8,000
- **LTV:CAC: 16.3x** ✅ Excellent unit economics

**Weighted Average (Mix: 40% Starter, 40% Team, 20% Enterprise):**
- **LTV:CAC: ~3.5x** ✅ Meets minimum threshold (3x)

**Target:** Improve to 4x+ by 2026 (raise Starter pricing, reduce Starter CAC)

---

- **Payback: 33 months** ⚠️ Too long (need 18-month target)

**Team Tier:**

**Enterprise Tier:**
- CAC: $8,000

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`

**The $18 Sweet Spot:** Our Team tier at $18/user/month hits a psychological pricing sweet spot. It's high enough to signal quality (not "cheap software") but low enough for rapid adoption (line item for most managers). At 50 users, that's $900/month or $10.8K/year — a discretionary budget item for most HR leaders, no CFO approval needed.

**Enterprise Price Elasticity:** Enterprise buyers are less price-sensitive than SMB buyers. A 1,000-user company choosing between $25 and $30/user/month cares more about security, support, and features than the $5K/month difference. This gives us significant pricing power at the Enterprise tier — we should test $35-40/user/month in 2026.

**The Starter Tier Problem:** At $12/user/month with $400 CAC and 33-month payback, Starter is not economically viable. We have three options: (1) Raise Starter to $15, (2) Reduce Starter CAC through product-led growth (virality, referrals), or (3) Accept Starter as a loss leader for Team/Enterprise upgrades. Option 3 is our current strategy — we'll re-evaluate in Q2 2025.
