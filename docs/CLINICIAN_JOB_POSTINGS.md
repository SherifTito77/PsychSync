## Job Posting #1: Crisis Intervention Clinician (Remote)

**Title:** Crisis Intervention Clinician - Remote - Save Lives with Innovative Mental Health Technology

**Company:** PsychSync
**Location:** Remote (USA)
**Type:** Part-Time Contract (On-Call)
**Compensation:** $75-125/hour + on-call stipend

---

### **About PsychSync**

PsychSync is a venture-backed mental health SaaS platform combining evidence-based clinical screening with automated crisis detection. We're on a mission to make mental health support accessible, proactive, and life-saving.

Our platform uses validated screening tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS) to identify individuals at risk and connect them with crisis intervention when needed. We're looking for compassionate clinicians to join our life-saving team.

---

### **The Role**

As a Crisis Intervention Clinician, you will:
- **Respond to automated suicide risk alerts** (within 5 minutes for critical cases)
- **Conduct telephonic risk assessments** with individuals in crisis
- **Provide compassionate crisis intervention** and safety planning
- **Make appropriate referrals** to emergency services or mental health providers
- **Document all interventions** in our HIPAA-compliant system
- **Participate in 24/7 on-call rotation** (1 week on, 2-3 weeks off)

---

### **Requirements**

**Must Have:**
- ✅ Current, unrestricted clinical license (LCSW, LMFT, LPCC, PhD, PsyD)
- ✅ 3-5+ years post-licensure experience
- ✅ Experience in crisis intervention, suicide assessment, or emergency psychiatry
- ✅ Ability to work independently under high-pressure situations
- ✅ Excellent clinical judgment and decision-making skills
- ✅ Reliable high-speed internet and private workspace

**Nice to Have:**
- 🌟 Certification in Crisis Intervention (CPI, ASIST)
- 🌟 Previous crisis line experience (988, Crisis Text Line)
- 🌟 Background in CBT, DBT, or trauma-informed care
- 🌟 Bilingual (Spanish/Mandarin/etc.)

---

### **What You'll Do**

**Typical Day On-Call:**
1. Receive automated alert for user who screened positive for suicide risk
2. Review screening results (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS scores)
3. Call user within 5 minutes (for critical alerts)
4. Conduct risk assessment (current plan, means, intent, protective factors)
5. Collaborate on safety plan and connect with resources
6. If imminent danger, contact emergency services or emergency contact
7. Document intervention in clinical notes

**Volume:**
- Average 5-15 alerts per day (varies by user base)
- Most calls take 15-30 minutes
- Critical alerts may require longer follow-up

---

### **Schedule**

- **On-Call Rotation:** 1 week on, 2-3 weeks off
- **Coverage:** 24/7 during your on-call week
- **Flexibility:** Work from home, sleep when no alerts
- **Backup:** Senior clinician available for consultation

**Example:**
- Week 1: ON-CALL (available 24/7, respond to alerts)
- Week 2-4: OFF (no obligations, live your life)
- Week 5: ON-CALL again
- Repeat

---

### **Compensation**

**Hourly Rate:** $75-125/hour (based on experience)

**Pay Structure:**
- **Standby Pay:** $25/hour (when on-call, not responding)
- **Response Stipend:** $100 per alert responded to
- **Night/Weekend Differential:** 1.5x hourly rate
- **Holiday Differential:** 2x hourly rate

**Estimated Earnings:**
- Average week: $1,500-2,500/week
- High-volume week: $2,500-4,000/week
- Monthly: $6,000-10,000 (depending on alert volume)

**Benefits:**
- Malpractice insurance provided
- Flexible remote work
- Continuing education stipend ($500/year)
- Clinical supervision included
- Save lives every day

**Flexibility:**
