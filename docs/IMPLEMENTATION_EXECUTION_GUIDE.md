## Risk Management & Contingency Planning

### High-Risk Scenarios & Mitigation Strategies

#### Risk 1: Slow Customer Acquisition
**Scenario**: Below-target discovery call booking rate
**Mitigation Strategies**:
```yaml
Immediate Response (Week 3):
  - Increase email outreach volume by 50%
  - Expand target audience to include additional industries
  - Offer free assessment or consultation as incentive
  - Leverage personal network for introductions

Medium-term Response (Week 4-5):
  - Review and optimize messaging and value proposition
  - Implement A/B testing on email subject lines and content
  - Add cold calling to outreach strategy
  - Consider temporary price reduction or special offer

Long-term Response (Week 6+):
  - Reassess target market and ideal customer profile
  - Invest in additional marketing channels
  - Explore partnership acquisition strategy
  - Consider hiring additional sales resources
```

#### Risk 2: Technical Implementation Delays
**Scenario**: Integration or security issues delaying customer onboarding
**Mitigation Strategies**:
```yaml
Technical Response:
  - Allocate additional engineering resources
  - Implement phased rollout with minimum viable features
  - Provide manual workarounds for critical functionality
  - Offer extended free trial period as compensation

Customer Communication:
  - Proactive communication with affected customers
  - Regular status updates and timeline revisions
  - Dedicated support team for affected customers
  - Value-add services to maintain engagement
```

#### Risk 3: Partnership Development Challenges
**Scenario**: Difficulty securing strategic partnership agreements
**Mitigation Strategies**:
```yaml
Partnership Strategy Adjustment:
  - Expand partnership criteria to include smaller firms
  - Offer more favorable commission rates for early partners
  - Develop pilot partnership programs with lower commitment
  - Focus on referral partnerships vs. strategic partnerships

Alternative Growth Channels:
  - Increase direct sales efforts
  - Expand paid advertising campaigns
  - Develop affiliate marketing program
  - Explore channel partnership with complementary software vendors
```
