# Customer Lifecycle and Touchpoints - PsychSync

## Executive Summary

This document defines the complete customer journey for PsychSync's psychological assessment SaaS platform, identifying all key touchpoints, conversion opportunities, and retention strategies across the customer lifecycle.

---

## Customer Lifecycle Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness & Discovery
**Duration:** 0-30 days before first contact
**Goal:** Generate qualified leads

**Touchpoints:**
- **Organic Channels**
  - Search engines (SEO: "personality assessment tools", "team analytics")
  - Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter thought leadership)
  - Content marketing (blog posts on psychology, team building)

- **Paid Channels**
  - Google Ads (high-intent keywords)
  - LinkedIn Ads (targeting HR managers, team leads)
  - Industry publications (Psychology Today, Harvard Business Review)

- **Referral Channels**
  - Customer referrals (incentivized)
  - Partner networks (HR consultants, executive coaches)
  - Conference presentations (psychology, HR tech events)

**Key Metrics:**
- Website traffic: 10,000 visitors/month
- Lead capture rate: 5% (500 leads/month)
- Cost per lead (CPL): <$50

---

### Stage 2: Consideration & Evaluation
**Duration:** 1-4 weeks
**Goal:** Convert leads to trial users

**Touchpoints:**
- **Website Experience**
  - Landing page: Value proposition video
  - Product tour: Interactive demo
  - Case studies: Success stories from similar companies
  - Pricing page: Transparent tier comparison

- **Content Engagement**
  - Whitepapers: "The Science of Team Psychology"
  - Webinars: "Building High-Performing Teams with Data"
  - Assessment samples: Free mini-assessments (MBTI, Big Five)

- **Sales Engagement**
  - Live chat: Sales bot qualification
  - Email nurture: 5-email educational sequence
  - Demo requests: Calendly scheduling
  - Sales calls: Consultative discovery

**Key Metrics:**
- Trial signup rate: 20% (100 trials/month)
- Demo attendance: 40% of trial signups
- Lead-to-opportunity: 30%

---

### Stage 3: Purchase & Onboarding
**Duration:** 2-8 weeks
**Goal:** Convert trials to paid customers

**Touchpoints:**

#### Pre-Purchase Phase
- **Trial Experience (14 days)**
  - Welcome email: Onboarding checklist
  - Interactive tutorial: Product walkthrough
  - Success manager outreach: Personalized setup assistance
  - Assessment invitations: Team onboarding

- **Evaluation Support**
  - Custom assessment creation: Show value
  - Team reports: Demonstrate insights
  - ROI calculator: Quantify benefits
  - Security documentation: Enterprise compliance

- **Purchase Decision**
  - Pricing negotiation: Enterprise discounts
  - Contract review: Legal approval process
  - Payment setup: Credit card/invoice options
  - E-signature: DocuSign integration

**Key Metrics:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 25%
- Average deal size: $5,000/year
- Sales cycle: 21 days average

---

### Stage 4: Adoption & Value Realization
**Duration:** First 90 days
**Goal:** Achieve time-to-first-value (TTFV) < 7 days

**Touchpoints:**

#### Week 1: Quick Start
- **Day 1: Welcome**
  - Onboarding email: Success roadmap
  - Customer success manager (CSM) introduction
  - Slack/Teams channel: Dedicated support

- **Day 3: First Assessment**
  - Guided assessment: MBTI for team
  - Results walkthrough: Interpret insights
  - Action planning: Apply findings

- **Day 7: Value Check**
  - Usage analytics: Team participation
  - ROI snapshot: Early wins
  - Best practices: Next steps

#### Week 2-4: Team Rollout
- **Team Configuration**
  - Organization setup: Departments/teams
  - User invitations: Bulk import
  - Assessment scheduling: Cadence planning

- **Training & Enablement**
  - Admin training: Platform management
  - User guides: Assessment interpretation
  - Video tutorials: Self-paced learning

#### Month 2-3: Expansion
- **Advanced Features**
  - Custom assessments: Tailored frameworks
  - Analytics dashboards: Team insights
  - Integrations: HRIS, Slack, Teams

- **Value Expansion**
  - Additional teams: Cross-organization rollout
  - Advanced frameworks: Beyond MBTI/Big Five
  - Executive reports: C-suite dashboards

**Key Metrics:**
- Time-to-first-value: 5 days
- User activation rate: 80%
- Feature adoption: 60% use advanced features

---

### Stage 5: Growth & Expansion
**Duration:** Ongoing (months 4-12)
**Goal:** Increase account value and depth

**Touchpoints:**

#### Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)
- **Month 3: First QBR**
  - Achievement review: Goals vs. actuals
  - ROI analysis: Quantified impact
  - Success stories: Internal champions
  - Expansion roadmap: Next 90 days

- **Month 6, 9, 12: Ongoing QBRs**
  - Trend analysis: Progress over time
  - Benchmarking: Industry comparisons
  - Upsell opportunities: Feature upgrades
  - Renewal planning: Contract terms

#### Expansion Opportunities
- **Seat Expansion**
  - New hires: Automated provisioning
  - Contractor access: Limited licenses
  - Alumni networks: Extended access

- **Feature Upgrades**
  - Enterprise features: SSO, advanced security
  - Custom frameworks: Bespoke assessments
  - AI insights: Predictive analytics
  - API access: Integration ecosystem

- **Strategic Partnerships**
  - Consulting engagements: Implementation services
  - Training programs: Certification courses
  - Research partnerships: Academic collaborations

**Key Metrics:**
- Net revenue retention (NRR): 120%
- Expansion revenue: 20% of existing ARR
- Upsell conversion: 30% of QBRs

---

### Stage 6: Retention & Advocacy
**Duration:** Ongoing (year 2+)
**Goal:** Maximize lifetime value (LTV) and referrals

**Touchpoints:**

#### Renewal Management
- **90 Days Before Renewal**
  - Value summary: Annual impact report
  - Usage analytics: Platform engagement
  - Success metrics: Goal achievement
  - Renewal quote: Early bird discounts

- **60 Days Before Renewal**
  - Stakeholder review: Executive alignment
  - Budget approval: Finance process
  - Contract negotiation: Terms & conditions

- **30 Days Before Renewal**
  - Final proposal: Signed quote
  - Implementation plan: Next-year roadmap
  - Payment processing: Invoice/receipt

#### Advocacy Programs
- **Customer Advocacy**
  - Case study participation: PR opportunities
  - Reference calls: Sales support
  - Conference speaking: Thought leadership
  - Advisory board: Product feedback

- **Referral Program**
  - Incentive structure: $500 credit per referral
  - Peer-to-peer sharing: LinkedIn integration
  - Partner network: HR consultants
  - Customer community: User groups

**Key Metrics:**
- Renewal rate: 95%
- Customer lifetime value (LTV): $25,000
- Referral rate: 20% of new customers
- Advocacy score: 70+ NPS

### Email Cadence

#### Trial Users (14 days)
- **Day 0:** Welcome + onboarding checklist
- **Day 1:** Quick start guide
- **Day 3:** First assessment reminder
- **Day 7:** Value check + tips
- **Day 10:** Final days warning
- **Day 13:** Trial expiration notice

#### New Customers (First 90 days)
- **Day 0:** Purchase confirmation + success roadmap
- **Week 1:** Weekly progress updates
- **Month 1:** Monthly success summary
- **Month 2:** Feature spotlight
- **Month 3:** QBR invitation

#### Existing Customers (Ongoing)
- **Weekly:** Product tips (Tuesday)
- **Monthly:** Newsletter + feature updates
- **Quarterly:** Business review + insights

#### Contextual Triggers
- **First login:** Tutorial activation
- **Assessment creation:** Best practices
- **Report generation:** Sharing tips
- **Feature discovery:** New capability highlights
- **Inactivity:** Re-engagement prompts

#### Success Milestones
- **First assessment completed:** Celebration modal
- **10 assessments:** Achievement badge
- **Team fully onboarded:** Progress milestone
- **Quarterly anniversary:** Impact summary

## Customer Journey Maps

### Persona A: HR Manager (Mid-Market Company)

**Awareness:**
- Searches "team personality assessment tools"
- Reads blog post "Building Data-Driven Teams"
- Downloads whitepaper "The Psychology of High Performance"

**Consideration:**
- Requests demo via website
- Attends product walkthrough
- Evaluates 3 competitors (PsychSync, Culture Amp, Gallup)

**Purchase:**
- Starts 14-day trial
- Onboards 15 team members
- Sees value in team insights
- Purchases 50-seat license ($5,000/year)

**Adoption:**
- Completes MBTI for entire team
- Shares insights at team meeting
- Expands to additional departments
- Renews for Year 2

### Persona B: Team Lead (Enterprise Company)

**Awareness:**
- Referred by colleague at another company
- Views case study "Fortune 500 Team Transformation"
- Attends webinar "Executive Team Analytics"

**Consideration:**
- Contacts sales for enterprise pricing
- Requests security documentation
- Evaluates integration options (Slack, Workday)

**Purchase:**
- Negotiates enterprise contract (500 seats)
- Requires SSO and custom assessments
- 3-month implementation timeline

**Adoption:**
- Rolls out to 10 teams
- Customizes frameworks for roles
- Integrates with HR systems
- Becomes internal champion
