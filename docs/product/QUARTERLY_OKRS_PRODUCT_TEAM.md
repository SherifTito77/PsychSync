# Quarterly OKRs - Product Team
## PsychSync - Q2 2025 (April - June)

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) for the PsychSync product team for Q2 2025. These OKRs align with company-wide goals to accelerate growth, improve user experience, and expand enterprise capabilities.

**Quarterly Themes:**
- **Growth:** Scale user acquisition and activation
- **Excellence:** Deliver exceptional user experience
- **Innovation:** Launch AI-powered insights
- **Enterprise:** Expand enterprise feature set

---

## Objective 1: Accelerate User Growth and Activation

**Strategic Priority:** Revenue Growth

### Key Results

#### KR 1.1: Increase trial signups by 150% (from 100 to 250 per month)
- **Current Baseline:** 100 trial signups/month
- **Target:** 250 trial signups/month

**Timeline:**
