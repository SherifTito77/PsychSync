## Email #3: Request for Specific Legal Opinion

**Subject:** Request for Legal Opinion: Crisis Intervention & Duty to Warn

**To:** attorney@lawfirm.com

**Body:**

Dear [Attorney Name],

Following our recent discussion, I'm writing to request a formal legal opinion on a specific scenario related to our crisis intervention services.

**Scenario:**

A user completes our PHQ-9 depression screening and scores 26/27 (severe depression), indicating active suicide ideation on item 9. Our system automatically:
1. Alerts our on-call crisis clinician
2. Clinician calls user within 5 minutes
3. If user doesn't answer, clinician calls emergency contact
4. If imminent danger believed, clinician contacts emergency services (911)

**Specific Questions:**

1. **Does our screening constitute "practicing medicine" or "practicing psychology" under state law?**
   - We use evidence-based tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS)
   - We do NOT provide diagnoses
   - We DO provide crisis intervention

2. **What is our duty to warn/duty to protect obligation?**
   - If user screens positive but we can't reach them
   - If user refuses intervention
   - If user is in different state than clinician

3. **Are we protected under the emergency exception to confidentiality?**
   - When can we contact family/friends?
   - When can we call 911 without user consent?
   - What documentation is required?

4. **What if we get the location wrong?**
   - User provided address is outdated
   - IP address location is inaccurate
   - What liability do we have for mistaken welfare checks?

5. **Are our disclaimers sufficient?**
   - "This is NOT emergency care"
   - "This is NOT a diagnostic tool"
   - "Call 988 or 911 if in crisis"

**Context:**

- We operate in all 50 states
- Crisis clinicians are licensed in their home states
- Users may be located in any state
- We have BAA with all vendors handling PHI
- All data is encrypted at rest and in transit

**Requested Format:**

We'd appreciate a written legal opinion addressing these questions that we can reference internally and share with our board/investors.

**Timeline:**

If possible, we'd need this by [date] to inform our product launch decisions.

Thank you for your guidance on these critical issues.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
PsychSync

## 📋 Attorney Selection Criteria

**When reviewing responses, look for:**

✅ **Experience with mental health law** (not just general healthcare)
✅ **Understanding of digital health/telehealth regulations**
✅ **Familiarity with HIPAA Security Rule** (not just Privacy Rule)
✅ **Knowledge of crisis intervention liability**
✅ **Experience with FDA SaMD** (Software as a Medical Device)
✅ **Responsive communication** (answered within 24-48 hours)
✅ **Reasonable fees** (compare 3+ quotes)

**Red Flags:**

❌ No mental health law experience
❌ Unfamiliar with telehealth regulations
❌ Says "you don't need to worry about X" without thorough review
❌ Poor communication (slow to respond, doesn't answer questions)
❌ Cannot provide examples of similar work
