## Creating Your Account

1. Go to [https://app.psychsync.com](https://app.psychsync.com)
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email address and create a secure password
4. Verify your email address (check your inbox for a verification link)
5. Complete your profile information

## Setting Up Your Organization

If you're the first person from your organization to join PsychSync:

1. After logging in, you'll be prompted to create an organization
2. Enter your organization name and description
3. Choose your organization size
4. Select your industry (optional)

## Creating Teams

Teams are the core unit for assessments and optimization in PsychSync:

1. Navigate to the "Teams" section from the main dashboard
2. Click "Create New Team"
3. Fill in team details:
   - Team name
   - Team description
   - Team purpose (optional)
4. Add team members by entering their email addresses
5. Assign roles to team members

### Team Roles

- **Team Lead**: Can manage team settings and view all results
- **Member**: Can participate in assessments and view their own results
- **Viewer**: Can only view team analytics (no assessments)

## Running Assessments

PsychSync offers multiple assessment frameworks:

### Available Frameworks

- **Big Five (OCEAN)**: Measures Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism
- **MBTI**: Myers-Briggs Type Indicator for personality preferences
- **Enneagram**: Nine personality types based on core motivations
- **Predictive Index**: Behavioral assessment for workplace fit

### Starting an Assessment

1. Go to your team's dashboard
2. Click "Start Assessment"
3. Choose the assessment framework
4. Select team members to assess
5. Set assessment timeline (optional)
6. Team members will receive email invitations to complete assessments

## Optimizing Teams

Once team members complete their assessments:

1. Navigate to your team dashboard
2. Click "Team Optimization"
3. Choose your optimization objective:
   - Maximize Engagement
   - Balance Personality Traits
   - Complementary Skills
   - Leadership Development
4. Review the AI-powered recommendations
5. Apply suggested changes or use as guidance for team restructuring

## Understanding Results

### Assessment Results

- **Individual Reports**: Detailed personality profiles for each team member
- **Team Composition**: Overview of team dynamics and traits
- **Strengths/Weaknesses**: Analysis of team capabilities

### Optimization Recommendations

- **Team Structure**: Suggestions for organizing team members
- **Collaboration Strategies**: Recommendations for working together
- **Development Areas**: Areas for team growth and improvement

## Best Practices

1. **Complete All Assessments**: Ensure all team members complete assessments for best results
2. **Regular Updates**: Reassess teams every 6-12 months
3. **Open Communication**: Share results with team members and discuss insights
4. **Action Planning**: Use recommendations to create concrete action plans
5. **Track Progress**: Monitor changes in team dynamics over time
